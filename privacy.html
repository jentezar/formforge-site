Privacy Policy

FormForge for iOS · Last updated October 6, 2026

FormForge is a fitness and nutrition app made by an independent developer. This page explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how to delete it.

In short: FormForge stores your training and nutrition data so it follows you between devices, sends small pieces of it to our server only when you use the AI features, shows no ads, sells no data, and does not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

What the app does not collect

  • No location.
  • No contacts, photo library, or browsing history.
  • No advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no ad networks in the app.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No audio recordings. Speech is transcribed on your device.

Apple Health data

Health data is read only with your permission and only for the types you approve. It is used to show your activity, sleep, and heart rate zones inside FormForge. If you use the daily insight feature, summarised figures such as a day's step count or hours slept are included in what is sent to our server to generate that insight. Health data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties for any purpose other than generating a feature you asked for. Nothing is written back to Apple Health.

The AI features

Label scanning, voice quick-log, recipe import, and the daily insight run on our server rather than on your phone. When you use one, the relevant input (a label photo, the transcribed text, a recipe link, or a summary of your recent training, nutrition, weight, and activity) is sent to our server and processed with Anthropic's Claude API under Anthropic's commercial terms. Inputs are used to return a result and are not retained by us afterwards. We keep a count of how many AI requests your account has made, which is how the free allowance works.

Where your data lives

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore, hosted in the United States) stores your account and app data. Security rules restrict access so that only you can read your own data, and friends can read only what you shared with them.
  • Vercel runs our server for the AI features.
  • Anthropic processes AI requests as described above.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status on Apple's behalf.

Each of these acts as a service provider on our instructions.

Sharing with other people

Other FormForge users can see your username if they search for it exactly, and nothing else unless you accept them as a friend. Friends see the workouts and notes you choose to share, and, only if you turn on "Share my progress", your Progress page. You can unfriend, block, or report anyone from inside the app. Blocking is private: the other person is not told.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. To delete your account, open Settings → Delete Account in the app. Apple asks you to sign in again to confirm, and then everything is removed: your training and nutrition history, profile, username, friendships, requests, blocks, shared workouts, and account record. Your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

The one exception is abuse reports. If another user has reported content you shared, the report itself is kept so that reporting cannot be undone by deleting the account it was about.

Children

FormForge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be deleted.
