FormForge

Fitness and nutrition for iPhone

Plan your training, log what you eat, and see the two side by side. FormForge reads your steps, sleep, and heart rate from Apple Health and shows them next to your workouts. Optional AI features read nutrition labels from a photo and log meals from a sentence.

Support

Email formforgeit@gmail.com. FormForge is made by one person, so you will get a reply from the person who built it.

Subscriptions

FormForge Premium unlocks the AI features. It is billed through Apple and managed in your Apple ID settings. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → FormForge. Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Everything is removed immediately. See the privacy policy for exactly what that covers.
